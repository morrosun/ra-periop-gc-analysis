In-Hospital Infection Risk Estimator

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) surgical patients in the ICU · companion tool to the multi-database cohort study

Inputs

Sequential Organ Failure Assessment at ICU admission
– predicted probability of in-hospital infection
Research use only — not for clinical decision-making. This tool is a transparent implementation of a multivariable logistic-regression model fitted on the MIMIC-IV ICU RA subgroup (n = 668) used in the companion cohort study. Predictors: any systemic glucocorticoid exposure during admission, age, sex, SOFA, and Charlson comorbidity index. The estimated association is observational; glucocorticoids are frequently administered to sicker patients (indication/confounding by severity), so the output reflects a risk profile, not a causal effect of the drug. External validation is pending.

Model

logit(p) = β₀ + βGC·GC + βage·age + βsex·sex + βSOFA·SOFA + βCharlson·Charlson
β₀ = −1.7189 · βGC = 0.6382 · βage = 0.0042 · βsex(M) = −0.4373 · βSOFA = 0.1697 · βCharlson = 0.0956
